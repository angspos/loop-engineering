Correction — 8 Sept 2026

A number I got wrong the first time

An earlier draft said Ng's reflection split moved GPT-4 from roughly 67% to 88% on HumanEval. That came from a secondary blog post and does not appear in Ng's writing. The figures above are his, from The Batch, 20 March 2024.

Two things to keep straight about them: the 95.1% is for agentic workflows in general, not for the Critic/Reviser split measured on its own — and it is a coding benchmark, not evidence about review panels. Cite it for the claim Ng actually makes: the workflow mattered more than the model upgrade.

Not from any of them

What this project invented

The five seats and their dials · the context packet and what it withholds · the triage split where verifiable findings skip the debate · the Actor / Critic / Reviser naming (Ng names the pattern Reflection and describes a separate critic; the three-role decomposition is the pattern literature's) · "confident garbage" as a phrase · the visual surface design · the five-rung ladder · fr-lint.js and every Fade Readers rule it checks.

These are argued from the sources above. They are not endorsed by them.
